Who We Are

The Chatpata Chat Story

Chatpata Chat started with a simple dream — to bring the real, honest flavours of Indian street food to the people of Rewari. Nestled right near the old bus stand, our small corner quickly became a beloved spot for locals, students, and anyone craving something genuinely tasty.

We are 100% vegetarian — always have been, always will be. Our kitchen uses no meat, no eggs, and no artificial flavour enhancers. Every ingredient is fresh, every recipe is crafted with care, and every order is made with a bit of love.

From crispy aloo tikki burgers to steaming hot momos, from classic pani puri to loaded noodles — at Chatpata Chat, every dish is a celebration of pure vegetarian taste. We believe great food doesn't have to be expensive, and a clean kitchen is non-negotiable.
