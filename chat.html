Our Ingredients & Purity Promise

At Chatpata Chat, purity isn't just a promise — it's our foundation. Every item on our menu is 100% vegetarian, prepared in a dedicated meat-free kitchen.

Farm-Fresh Vegetables Daily

We source fresh vegetables every morning from local markets. No frozen or pre-cut produce — everything is cut fresh on the day of use.

Hygienic Preparation Standards

Our kitchen is sanitised multiple times daily. Cooks maintain strict personal hygiene and use gloves for all food preparation.

No MSG – Natural Spices Only

We never use artificial flavour enhancers. All spice blends are prepared in-house from whole spices for genuine, natural flavour.

Quality Assured Every Batch

Every dish is taste-tested before it leaves the kitchen. We believe in consistency — your 10th order should taste as good as your first.
